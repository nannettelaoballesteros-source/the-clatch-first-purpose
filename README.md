## 1. System Overview
The Clatch™ First-Purpose™ is a life-safety mechanical decoupling apparatus engineered specifically for emergency medical services, search-and-rescue teams, and structural firefighters. Originating from Delta Nannette Omega - Yeoman LLC (DNO-Y) and open-sourced via the BNG Global Humanitarian Foundation (BNG-GHF), the Clatch™ eliminates bare-skin freeze adhesion down to minus 40 degrees Celsius and provides flashover thermal shielding exceeding 800 degrees Celsius without compromising protective nitrile gloves or operator dexterity. Commercial and industrial mechanics remain under separate license with Ballesteros Ballast Infrastructure LLC (BBI).

---

## 2. Core Functional Features
* **Instant Decoupling Under Load:** Rapid mechanical release under dynamic tension without requiring fine motor skills.
* **Photoluminescent High-Visibility Index:** Intrinsic luminescence for zero-visibility structural extrication.
* **Thermal Choke Barrier:** Mitigates extreme cryogenic and pyric thermal transfer between machinery and operator.
